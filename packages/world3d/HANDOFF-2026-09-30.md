## Run and judge

- Build: `WORLD3D_COURSES=<repo>/dist/courses node build.mjs` from `packages/world3d` (or `npm run build` in a checkout that has built courses). Serve `dist/` with any static server; word audio expects `courses/zh-china/audio/` next to it (symlink `content/audio/zh` when serving a bare `dist/`).
- Flags: `?look=full|lite|classic`, `?perf=1` (frame times, prefetch state, shadow stats), `?prefetch=0` (A/B the stall fix), `?collide=1` (collider outlines), `?env=` layer toggles, `?fps=30`.
- Tests: `npx vitest run packages/world3d --maxWorkers=1`. One known failure, `start.test` "the chrome follows the reading language" (`navigator is not defined`, predates this push). `textures.test` "against the library" needs `WORLD3D_LIBRARY` pointing at the asset library; set it to `/nonexistent` to skip.
- Captures: `scripts/look-capture.mjs` (`SET=tiers|env|textured|interiors|phone-hud`, `PORTRAIT=1`, `ROOMS=`), `scripts/motion-capture.mjs` (NPC head traces), `scripts/shadow-capture.mjs`, `scripts/collide-capture.mjs`. Reference shots per slice live under `shots/`.
- GitHub Pages deploys from `main` only (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`). Pushing this branch does not publish; merge to `main` or run the workflow by hand when the owner wants it public.

## Known bugs (do not fix without asking; the owner has seen these)

1. **Pavilion see-through hides the whole object.** The small open pavilion with the little roofs east of the plaza is not a room. When the player walks under it the see-through is supposed to fade only the roof and leave the floor, pillars and rail visible, but the whole pavilion fades out. The owner asked for this to be left as is for now. Where to look: `src/seethrough.ts` (which parts of an asset get a see-through slot) and the pavilion's part naming in the library set; the fix is per-part slots for roof pieces only, not per-asset.
2. Pavilion floor: the player's feet sink 0.3–0.4 m into its deck because the ground height under it is 0 (predates this push; collision now treats the deck as a walkable platform, see README "Movement and collision").
3. Interior right-hand wall is painted in the left wall's colour bands instead of loading its wall GLB (the `+x` wall assets are only in the library). Looks plainer than the other walls.
4. Prefetch builds rooms only in busy windows (dialogue, menus, title). A player who walks straight to a door with no busy moment gets the build behind the 300 ms loading hold, as before.
5. Build total is 15.8 MB (binary MB), right at the line the perf pass set. Any more assets means dropping something: music or texture density (buildings are 28 px/m).
6. Full tier is still about 2 ms GPU over its target on the owner's PC; phone heat on the Nord CE 5 was never measured on the device.
7. Noodle shop AC casings share the wall-tile material and can pick up a faint tile grid from the library's wear pass.
8. Long frames of 1–2 s right after the loading screen were seen on a heavily loaded capture machine, with and without prefetch. Not reproduced on a quiet machine; re-measure before chasing.
9. Only apple, cup and book can be bought in the shop; water, tea and vegetables open their word card (that is all the core's shop scene sells today).

## Decisions that stand

- Engine stays three.js; the toy look was assets + lighting, not the engine.
- Interior view is the fixed per-room camera at the street pitch. The top-down "whole room from above the ceiling" view was rejected.
- The player character's animation is the original baked clips. The procedural animator is for NPCs, walkers and pets only; a test enforces that the player never gets one.
- Textures are one atlas per asset from the library, then packed per space at runtime. Ground, grass, water, sky and canopy stay flat and are shaded by the game.
- Commit trailers are mixed (`Claude Fable 5.1` on some, `Claude Opus 5.5` on others). Cosmetic; left as is.
