# Performance after

Generated by scripts/perf-report.mjs from shots/perf/before.json, shots/perf/after-paced.json, shots/perf/after-uncapped.json, shots/perf/after-30.json. Same machine, harness and spots as shots/perf/before.md (Vega 11, ANGLE Vulkan, headless Chrome, vsync off, a fresh browser per row, 10 s per row, stats after the first second). Before had no frame cap: its rows are uncapped. After ships a 60 fps cap: "after (60 cap)" is what a player gets; "after (uncapped)" (`fps=0`) is the work per frame, the like-for-like comparison with before. Phone rows: 1080x2400 at DPR 2.6, emulated pixels drawn by the Vega 11 (no phone GPU).

## Reading it

- The shipping build caps at 60 fps: every Full and Lite row has 0 frames over 50 ms (max 28-31 ms, before: 387-1346 ms); with vsync on (below) Full's 1080p mean is 17.3-17.9 ms (GPU 13.8-14.3 ms a frame: over the 12 ms target), every other row 16.7 ms flat.
- The before rows' long frames were the see-through's synchronous read-back (`sync read-back`, up to 1346 ms) and, uncapped, a GPU queue run seconds deep that stalled a random GL call (`cpu: colour / shadow / bloom`). The uncapped after rows still show the second kind (GPU-bound with vsync and the cap off: a harness artifact; a 60 Hz browser never queues that deep) and no sync read at all; their GPU / CPU ms per frame are the work.
- Timer query ms on this APU move with its clock (DVFS) and run to run by 10-20 %: compare columns within one capture.
